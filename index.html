How SWABUD works

Nothing goes live until the official page confirms it

Social posts and forwarded images are only leads. A listing is published after we check it at the source, and it carries the date we last checked.

  1. Find the official source

    The organizer's own website, verified account or official email. A reposted poster is not a source.

  2. Confirm the facts

    Organizer, eligibility, dates, format, cost and the real application link.

  3. Publish with a check date

    Statuses then switch on their own, in Nepal time: Opens in, Open, Closing soon, Happening now and Closed. We recheck open listings every week.

Five signs an opportunity is fake

  • You must pay to "reserve a seat" or "confirm your selection".
  • No official website, page or named contact can be found.
  • It asks for citizenship copies, bank details or passwords to apply.
  • It promises a guaranteed job or a prize with no clear rules.
  • The only source is a forwarded image or message.
