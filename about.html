About SWABUD

SWABUD is a student tech opportunity platform for Nepal. We bring scattered opportunities into one organized, checked place, and help students act on them. Discover. Connect. Grow.

Why SWABUD exists

Opportunities shouldn't depend on which group chat you are in

Hackathons, CTFs, fellowships, scholarships and events for students are announced across Facebook posts, college notice boards, Discord servers and dozens of websites. Students miss deadlines, cannot tell real calls from scams, and often don't know where to start, especially outside Kathmandu valley.

SWABUD collects these opportunities, checks each one against its official source, writes it up in one clear format, and keeps it updated until it closes. Then it helps you act: save it, plan your application, and get reminded before the deadline.

Our name carries our promise: Discover what's out there, Connect with the people and communities behind it, and Grow the skills to be chosen.

What we do

Six things SWABUD does for students

125 listings across 8 categories, 85 of them confirmed free, plus learning paths, career guides and a personal dashboard.

Finds opportunities

We watch official platforms, college notices, community pages and organizer announcements for hackathons, CTFs, fellowships, scholarships, events, courses, volunteering and student programmes.

Checks them at the source

A post is only a lead. A listing goes live after we confirm the organizer, eligibility, dates, format, cost and the real application link on the official page.

Keeps them up to date

Statuses and countdowns switch by themselves in Nepal time: Opens in, Open, Closing soon, Happening now and Closed. We recheck open listings every week.

Helps you apply

Members save opportunities, track each application from Saved to Accepted, keep a checklist and notes, and get a heads-up before deadlines.

Builds your skills

Free learning paths and practical career guides, from your first CV to hackathon prep and spotting fake offers.

Connects the community

Partner communities, campus ambassadors and organizers across Nepal, so opportunities reach students outside the big cities too.

How it works

For students

  1. Browse

    See a preview of open opportunities, with statuses and countdowns, without an account.

  2. Create a free account

    Sign up with Google or email in under a minute to unlock every listing, the calendar, sources and guides.

  3. Save and plan

    Save what fits, tick off the application checklist, and watch your deadlines on your dashboard.

  4. Apply at the source

    Every application happens on the organizer's official page. SWABUD never asks you to pay to apply.

How it works

For organizers

For organizers

  1. Submit

    Send an opportunity from your SWABUD account, or use the partner form for collaborations.

  2. We verify

    We check it against your official page and may email you with questions.

  3. It goes live

    The listing is published with a check date, a live status and a countdown.

  4. Students find it

    It appears in the directory, the calendar, recommendations and our weekly alerts.

How we verify

Nothing goes live until the official page confirms it

Reposts and forwarded images are only leads. We confirm every fact at the source, publish it with a check date, correct mistakes quickly and note every correction.

What SWABUD will never do

  • Charge students to see listings or to use their account
  • Sell or share student data
  • Promise jobs, visas or selection
  • Present a paid listing as an independent recommendation
  • Ask for your password, OTP or bank details

Questions

About SWABUD, answered

Still curious? Write to info@swabud.tech or use the contact form.

Is SWABUD free?

Yes. Browsing, the account and every member feature are free for students. Some listed opportunities, such as certification exams, charge their own fees, and each listing says so.

Why do I need an account to see everything?

An account lets us keep your saved list, tracker and progress in one place, and helps us keep the platform useful and free of spam. It's free and takes under a minute.

Does SWABUD run these opportunities?

No. Organizers run them. We check the details and send you to the official page to apply.

Who is SWABUD for?

Students in Nepal who are interested in technology, from first-year BCA, BIT, BSc CSIT and BE students to fresh graduates, plus the clubs, colleges and companies that run genuine opportunities.

How can my club or company work with SWABUD?

List an opportunity for free, or partner with us for events, sessions and outreach. Start on the For organizers page.
